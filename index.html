    Folio Reader

    Version 1.1.0

    A simple PDF reader for viewing, sharing and printing. It works offline, and your files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

    Install it as an app

    • Android: open the browser menu and tap Install app or Add to Home screen. Then you can share PDFs straight to Folio Reader from WhatsApp, Gmail or Files.
    • iPhone/iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
    • Computer: click the install icon in Chrome or Edge's address bar. You can then open PDFs with Folio Reader from your files.

    PDF rendering by PDF.js (Mozilla, Apache 2.0 licence).
    Drop a PDF to open it
    To print a PDF, open it in Folio Reader and use the print button.
